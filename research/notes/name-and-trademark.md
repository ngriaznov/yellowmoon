**Yellowmoon pre-launch name check (11 October 2026)**
This is due diligence, not legal advice. Before filing a mark or committing to the name, get a clearance search from a trademark attorney (conseil en propriété industrielle).

**Verdict: CAUTION, leaning clear.** The US register has no YELLOWMOON or YELLOW MOON mark in class 9 or 42, live or dead, and I found no software product with the name. The caution is because I couldn't query EUIPO/TMview, WIPO or INPI, and those matter most for a developer in France. Also, yellowmoon.com, yellowmoon.io and the GitHub username are already taken. The closest user in the same field is Yellow Moon Post Production Ltd in Northern Ireland: film and TV post-production with a grading room and VFX, trading since 1996 at yellowmoon.net.

**Registers I could not check, and how to check them manually**
1. **EUIPO / TMview:** the page is a JavaScript app and returned an empty shell. At tmdn.org/tmview, search "yellowmoon" and "yellow moon" and filter to classes 9 and 42 for the EU, France, the UK and WIPO.
2. **WIPO Global Brand Database:** the query URL was rejected and the site is JavaScript. At branddb.wipo.int, search the brand name with fuzzy matching in classes 9 and 42.
3. **INPI (data.inpi.fr):** a JavaScript shell, and robots.txt blocks the mark pages. Search Marques for both spellings. Two gazette pages (FR4063480, FR4573050) came up in a web search but don't show the mark text. They are in classes 33 and 14 and remain unverified [23].
4. **UK IPO:** the fetch failed. It matters because of the Northern Ireland firm. Search at trademarks.ipo.gov.uk.
5. **USPTO direct search:** I used the Justia mirror plus TSDR. To confirm, run "yellow moon" and "yellow*moon*" in classes 9 and 42 at tmsearch.uspto.gov.
6. **App and code sites:** the App Store, Google Play, Product Hunt and GitHub repo search blocked automated access. Search them by hand.
7. **Domains:** confirm .app, .dev and .photo at a registrar before relying on them.

# What it takes to move the Yellowmoon interface to GPUI

For Mykyta, 2026-10-19. Research only: this file and one row in `research/README.md` are the only changes; nothing was built into the app. It is the companion of `research/egui-port-2026-10-19.md` (same day, same questions) and follows `research/gui-toolkit-2026-10-16.md`, which gave GPUI three lines ("clean licence, strong macOS feel, unstable API and no wgpu sharing. Not now"). Everything external was read on 2026-10-19; sources are tagged `[G1]`, `[G2]` and listed at the end. There is no Mac here: every statement about how something looks or feels on macOS comes from source code, documentation and issue threads, not from running it. Licence remarks are my reading of the texts, not legal advice.

What stays the same whatever the toolkit is not repeated here; the egui report has it: the inventory of what exists (its section 1: 25.4k lines of TypeScript, 4.1k of CSS, 18.3k of Vitest, 5.6k of Playwright to rewrite; all of `crates/*` and about 6k lines of `src-tauri` carried over), the split of `src-tauri` into a toolkit-free library crate first (its 2.1), the non-blocking UI model over `AppApi` (2.2), the bundle script, the updater module and moving installed 0.1.x copies over (7.2, 7.3), and the specs and rules that change (9).

## 0. Short answer

1. **The licence is clean, but GPUI does not come from Zed.** GPUI and every Zed crate it needs are Apache-2.0 [G2, G4]. Zed has not published `gpui` to crates.io since 0.2.2 (2025-10-22), which predates the platform split, AccessKit and pinch [G1, G2]. Current GPUI reaches crates.io as **`gpui-pre`**: a weekly snapshot of Zed's main branch (0.3.9 is zed@93d7456, published 2026-10-12), renamed crate by crate and published by huacnlee, the author of the `gpui-component` library, from a script in that library's repository [G1, G6]. A breaking snapshot is a patch bump, so the version must be pinned with `=` [G6]. A git dependency on Zed is ruled out by `deny.toml` and would build a dependency set Zed never tests.
2. **The dependency tree is allowed but big.** A throwaway crate with `gpui-pre =0.3.9`, `gpui-pre-platform =0.3.9` (`font-kit` feature) and `gpui-component =0.8.0` resolves to 490 shipped crates on macOS (486 on Linux with the platform's windowing features off), all on the `deny.toml` allow list; 182 crates or versions would be new to `Cargo.lock`. Without `gpui-component` it is 364. egui's tree was 203 (section 8.1).
3. **Where GPUI is ahead of egui for this app.**
   - *Text:* Core Text shapes and rasterises on macOS, so the text looks native. OpenType features reach Core Text, so `tnum` works as it is [G2]. Core Text falls back to system fonts for ⌘ ⇧ ⌥ ↩ →, which Figtree lacks.
   - *Controls:* `gpui-component` (Apache-2.0, weekly releases) has nearly every glasscn control we use, with Lucide icons (the family `lucide-react` gives us now) [G3]. GPUI's styling is Tailwind-shaped (`div().flex().gap_3().rounded_md().shadow_lg()`), so our glasscn and Tailwind layout ports more literally than to egui.
   - *Colour:* Zed main added `WindowOptions::use_display_p3` on 2026-10-16 (PR #64394) [G4, G5]. Once a `gpui-pre` snapshot carries it (0.3.9 does not), the window can be Display P3 with no patch of ours.
   - *Mac shell:* native menus validated by action availability, native open, save and alert panels, Finder drops with a hover state, a transparent title bar with movable traffic lights, ProMotion at 120 Hz [G2, G9].
4. **Where it is behind.**
   - *Variable-font weights:* a variable font is one face, and the weight is picked among faces, so Figtree's `wght` axis is never set [G2]. Ship static instances (the six weights the interface uses) as one family.
   - *Letter-spacing:* `TextStyle` has none [G2].
   - *Gradients:* linear only, with two stops [G2].
   - *Clipping and blur:* no rounded clipping of children, no backdrop blur.
   - *The photo:* no way to run our own GPU code or show an arbitrary GPU texture. A GPUI image per frame (through the sprite atlas) is ruled out as too slow (your answer of 2026-10-19). Two routes remain: a `surface()` of our own `CVPixelBuffer`s once a small patch lets it take BGRA (as shipped it takes only 4:2:0 YCbCr video and would blur the colour), or our own Metal view under a transparent GPUI window, as today [G2] (section 3).
   - *Input and quit:* no smart-zoom event; momentum scrolling arrives but cannot be told from finger movement; quit handlers get 200 ms and quit cannot be delayed [G2].
   - *Accessibility:* it shipped in May 2026 (PR #56065) and is on by default, but Zed itself still turns it off. `gpui-component` gives no roles to its slider, switch, select or dialog [G2, G3, G5].
   - *Tests:* no snapshot or golden-image testing comparable to egui_kittest.
5. **Size.** About 17.5 agent-days to parity (range 13 to 27), plus 6 to 10 round trips with your Mac. That is a day and a half fewer than egui's 19, because the controls and layout port faster; the risk is higher, because the dependency comes from a third party every week (section 8).
6. **Decisions before anything starts** (section 10): the same two settled decisions as for egui (the Tauri shell, glasscn), plus whether a weekly third-party snapshot of Zed's GPUI is an acceptable kind of dependency.

## 1. Getting GPUI

- **`gpui` on crates.io (0.2.2, 2025-10-22): missing.** It is from before the split of the platform code into its own crates, has no AccessKit and no pinch, and still starts apps with `Application::new()` [G1, G2]. Zed main still declares 0.2.2 with `publish = true`; I found no 2026 statement from Zed about publishing, and gpui.rs says "For the near future, GPUI is tied to Zed" [G4, G8].
- **`gpui-pre` (0.3.9, 2026-10-12): works today.** `script/bump-gpui.ts` in longbridge/gpui-kit, run every Monday by `release-gpui.yml` on a macOS runner, collects every internal crate behind `gpui`, `gpui_platform` and `gpui_macros` from a Zed commit, renames each to `gpui-pre-<name>` (keeping the library names, so the code says `use gpui::*`), swaps Zed's git forks of `reqwest` and `wgpu` for crates.io versions with marked source patches, refuses copyleft crates, and records the Zed commit in `[package.metadata.gpui-pre]` [G6]. docs.rs builds it. The platform backends are separate crates: `gpui-pre-platform` picks `gpui-pre-macos` (with `gpui-pre-apple`, the Metal renderer and Core Text) on a Mac [G2].
- **Alternatives.** `gpui-unofficial` (1.24.3, 2026-10-19) republishes Zed's release tags under renamed crates, with AccessKit and pinch [G7]: a fallback if `gpui-pre` stops. `gpui-ce` (a community fork) has yanked its 0.3 releases and is diverging from upstream: not recommended [G7].
- **Churn.** 0.2 to 0.3 was a large break (the platform split, `gpui_platform::application()`). The weekly bumps since are mostly small: gpui-kit's 0.3.9 upgrade says "No library code changes" apart from tests [G6]. Structs keep gaining fields, so every construction ends in `..Default::default()`. `gpui-component` pins one exact `gpui-pre`, so the two move together, every week if we let them.
- **Build.** The Metal shaders are compiled with `xcrun metal`, which needs full Xcode (not only the command-line tools) unless the `runtime_shaders` feature is on [G2]. Stable Rust; gpui-kit asks for 1.92 or newer, our toolchain is 1.97.

## 2. How a GPUI Yellowmoon would be put together

The crate split, the CLI, the parity of app and `ym`, and the non-blocking UI model are as in the egui report, section 2. What differs:

- **Entities and views.** GPUI is retained: each screen and panel is an `Entity` that implements `Render`, holds its state and calls `cx.notify()` when it changes; only views that were notified are rebuilt [G2, G8]. That is close to the React components of `src/screens/`, with the state of `store.ts` split among a few entities (session, current photo's settings, look library, export run, UI state).
- **Threads.** `cx.background_spawn` runs `AppApi` calls on GPUI's executor, and the foreground task that awaits them updates the entity. Core events arrive through a channel drained by a foreground task, which calls `notify` on the views that care. GPUI repaints only what changed, on its display link [G2].
- **Styling.** Tailwind-shaped builder methods, flexbox and grid through Taffy, absolute positioning [G2]. Grid columns must be equal widths [G2], so the slider row (`92px 1fr 72px 40px`) and the Photos grid (`minmax(0, 1fr) 400px`) become flex rows with fixed and flexible children. The tokens of `design/tokens.json` become a `gpui-component` theme (JSON, 135 colours with alpha, radii, shadows, fonts, focus ring) plus constants for our own panes [G3].
- **Keys.** Actions and key bindings with contexts (`"Photos && !Input"`) replace `src/state/shortcuts.ts`: a binding scoped to a context that a text field leaves does not fire while the field has focus. Single keys must be scoped that way, or they fire inside text fields [G2].

## 3. The photo

GPUI's set of drawable primitives is fixed: no paint hook, no element that takes an arbitrary GPU texture, no access to its Metal device [G2]. The one element for externally made pixels, `surface()`, is narrower than it sounds (the surface route below). Three ways in principle; you ruled the first out, so two remain:

- **Ruled out: a GPUI image per frame** (your answer of 2026-10-19: "would be too slow, this is not valid route"). Each frame would be a new `RenderImage` (BGRA), copied into the sprite atlas as a texture of its own (a 2880 × 1920 frame is larger than the 1024² atlas pages), kept until `drop_image` frees it, after an RGBA-to-BGRA swizzle: a new 22 MB texture and a 22 MB copy for every frame [G2]. GPUI images stay right for what changes rarely: the filmstrip thumbnails and the look tiles (section 4.4).
- **The surface route (recommended): a `surface()` fed with our own `CVPixelBuffer`s, after a patch.** `surface()` and its `PaintSurface` primitive exist to composite natively made pixels: on macOS the renderer turns a `CVPixelBuffer` into Metal textures through a `CVMetalTextureCache`, with no copy into the atlas. As it ships it is built for Zed's screen sharing, not for photos: it `assert_eq!`s that the buffer is `kCVPixelFormatType_420YpCbCr8BiPlanarFullRange` (anything else panics), samples the luma and the 2 × 2-subsampled chroma planes, converts with a fixed full-range BT.601 matrix in `shaders.metal`, and ignores the buffer's colour tags (`gpui_apple/src/metal_renderer.rs`, `draw_surfaces`, the same in `gpui-pre-apple` 0.3.9 and on Zed main read 2026-10-19) [G2, G4]. Feeding it our frames as is would halve the colour resolution and shift colours by an 8-bit YCbCr round trip: not acceptable for judging a photo at 100 %. (On Windows, `draw_surfaces` is an empty function on Zed main and `PaintSurface` carries no image there; irrelevant to us, and it contradicts summaries that say Windows surfaces work.) **Not supported yet, but the patch exists as an open pull request.** The newest `gpui-pre-apple` (0.3.9, fetched again on 2026-10-19; no newer snapshot was published) and Zed main read the same day name one pixel format, the 4:2:0 one, in the surface assert and in a unit test; every other BGRA in the crate is the window layer, the atlas, offscreen textures or the screenshot readback. Zed PR #61291, "gpui: Support single-plane BGRA CVPixelBuffers in surface rendering" (opened 2026-07-19, not a draft, two files: `metal_renderer.rs` and `shaders.metal`), makes `draw_surfaces` branch on the format: `kCVPixelFormatType_32BGRA` binds one `BGRA8Unorm` texture through a new `surfaces_bgra` pipeline and a `surface_fragment_bgra` function that samples **nearest-neighbour** ("a surface is presented at its native device-pixel size, so filtering can only blur it"), and unknown formats are logged and skipped instead of aborting [G5]. Its author measured, with 1200 × 1602 @2x BGRA IOSurfaces at 60 fps from an off-screen renderer, about 6 % of a core against 25 to 40 % for the same frames through `img()` and `RenderImage`, which supports your ruling on the image route (their numbers, not independently checked). It has had no review in three months: a Zed engineer wrote on 2026-09-29 that he would look at it [G5]. A second open draft, PR #65563 (2026-10-13), fixes a race that our design would hit: `draw_surfaces` drops its Core Video textures while the command buffer is still being encoded, so a `CVPixelBufferPool` can hand out a buffer the GPU is still sampling (reproduced 20 times in 20 on main on an M4 Max, macOS 26.5); it also turns the abort on an unsupported format inside an open encoder into a skip [G5]. So the patch is those two pull requests' changes to `gpui-pre-apple` (Apache-2.0 like the crate), vendored under `[patch.crates-io]` until they are merged and reach a `gpui-pre` snapshot; without #65563 our own code must keep each buffer out of the pool until the frame after next has been presented. Nearest-neighbour sampling is right at 1:1 and makes the fast pan and zoom below blocky while a zoom is in flight, until the sharp frame lands. With the patch it is the right primitive: frames go into IOSurface-backed buffers from a `CVPixelBufferPool` (one CPU copy into a reused buffer, no atlas, no new texture per frame), and later, zero-copy, our wgpu device renders into a Metal texture made on the same IOSurface (through wgpu-hal), with double buffering so GPUI never samples a frame being written. The colour of the photo then follows the layer (`use_display_p3`), not the buffer's tags. `paint_surface` snaps its bounds to device pixels and the surface shader clips to the content mask (`window.rs` line 5155, `surface_vertex` in `shaders.metal`) [G2], so the fast pan and zoom of the last frame (egui report, 2.3) is the same buffer painted at shifted and scaled bounds inside the photo cell's mask, every display frame, while the core renders the sharp one. Each new photo frame asks GPUI for a frame of the window, which G2 measures. The patch has to be carried over at every `gpui-pre` upgrade until it is upstream. Two other upstream efforts may later replace it: PR #64061 (open, 2026-09-11) adds `DynamicTexture`, a stable BGRA texture per element updated in place by dirty region on Metal, wgpu and DirectX (still a CPU upload, but no new texture per frame), and PR #60573 proposed zero-copy composition of wgpu textures rendered on GPUI's own device; it was closed on 2026-07-08 because "the implementation is currently ahead of the design conversation", "not as a rejection of the idea", and its Metal side was a placeholder [G5].
- **The native-view route (fallback): our own Metal view under a transparent GPUI window.** What `src-tauri/src/native.rs` does today under WKWebView: an `NSView` with a wgpu surface in Display P3 below GPUI's view, GPUI's background transparent where the cell is (`WindowBackgroundAppearance::Transparent`), the cell's bounds sent to the view after each layout. It keeps our device, our present, P3 on every snapshot and no atlas traffic; GPUI's menus and tooltips still draw over the photo, because GPUI's layer is on top. It also keeps the compositing through a hole, which is what the move to a toolkit could otherwise remove, and how GPUI's view shares the window with ours is unverified.

Recommendation: the surface route, with the BGRA patch from the first day of the spike (no atlas traffic, a path to zero-copy, everything drawn by GPUI); the native-view route only if the patch cannot be made to pass G2.

## 4. Rendering: colour, glass, text, widgets

### 4.1 Colour

- In 0.3.9 the window's Metal layer is `BGRA8Unorm` with no colour space set, which Apple documents as "not color-matched": our pixels go to the display unconverted [G2, G11]. On the built-in P3 panel a P3 frame then looks right and the sRGB UI colours look a little too saturated; on an sRGB display the reverse. Measure it in the spike.
- Blending is in gamma-encoded space, like a browser, so translucent panes should match the C2 screenshots; hex colours pass through unchanged [G2].
- **Display P3: works with a newer snapshot.** `WindowOptions::use_display_p3` (macOS only, off by default) is on Zed main since PR #64394 (merged 2026-10-16, closing #8293); it tags the layer with `kCGColorSpaceDisplayP3` (checked in `crates/gpui_apple/src/metal_renderer.rs` on main) [G4, G5]. Until a `gpui-pre` snapshot carries it, the same twenty lines can be patched into `gpui-pre-apple` through `[patch.crates-io]` with a path. With it on: the viewport stays in Display P3 as today, the greys of the glass do not change, and only the yellow and the tab colours need converting once.
- No HDR or EDR: output is 8-bit [G2].

### 4.2 The glass

- **Works today:** corner radii per corner, borders per side (one colour), several Gaussian box shadows per element with offset, blur, spread and inset, opacity, `canvas()` for our own painting, `PathBuilder` fills and strokes with 4× multisampling [G2]. Better shadows than egui's.
- **Gradients are linear with two stops**; radial and conic are missing [G2]. The aurora's blobs, the wheels' hue ring and the warp plane's hue field become images rendered in Rust (the warp field is already an image today).
- **Opacity is per primitive**, not per group [G3]: fading a whole composite control (a locked button with its label and icon) fades each part separately, which shows where they overlap.
- **No rounded clipping of children** (masks are rectangles; quads and images have their own radii) and **images cannot be tinted** (SVG icons can) [G2].
- **No backdrop blur.** `WindowBackgroundAppearance::Blurred` blurs the desktop behind the window, not our content [G2]. The route is the one the egui report recommends, and GPUI has a call for it: `paint_image(bounds, image_bounds, corner_radii, …)` draws the part of an image that falls in `bounds`, with rounded corners. Each pane paints the one window-sized, pre-blurred (28 px, with saturate and brightness) aurora with the window as `image_bounds` and itself as `bounds`, so every pane shows exactly the blurred ground behind it, from one texture made once per window size and scale [G2]. Menus and dialogs over the photo or the panel get a more opaque fill.

### 4.3 Text

- **Native text.** On macOS, shaping is `CTLine` and rasterising `CGContext`, so the text looks like a Mac app's [G2]. The `font-kit` feature of `gpui-pre-platform` must be on; without it the Mac gets a text system that draws nothing (issue #63768, open) [G2, G5].
- **Fonts from bytes:** TTF and OTF through `add_fonts`; woff2 unverified, so ship TTF (Figtree declares no Reserved Font Name; checked) [G2].
- **Weights: missing for variable fonts.** The weight is matched among the faces of a family and a variable font is one face, so `FontWeight(600.)` gets the default instance (`gpui-pre-apple-0.3.9/src/text_system.rs`, `find_best_match`; issue #64783 reports the same on Linux) [G2, G5]. Ship static instances of Figtree at the six weights the interface uses (400, 500, 600, 650 for the glass headings, 700, 800; `src/styles/`) made by a `tools/` script with fontTools' instancer (OFL 1.1 allows it), registered as one family.
- **Tabular figures: works today.** `FontFeatures` goes to Core Text, so `tnum` in the value boxes is one line [G2].
- **Letter-spacing: missing.** `TextStyle` has no tracking [G2]. The caps labels (0.05em in the mockups) lose their tracking, or a tracked face is generated with the static instances, or GPUI is patched.
- **Symbols:** Core Text falls back to system fonts for glyphs Figtree lacks; explicit fallback lists also work [G2]. That the system's ⌘ ⇧ ⌥ ↩ → ▾ ✓ are the ones we want is a spike check (it is what WebKit shows today).
- Labels are not selectable unless an element makes them so; `gpui-base` has a selectable text element for the few texts that should be [G3].

### 4.4 Our drawn widgets and lists

| Widget | In GPUI |
|---|---|
| Colour wheels | the hue disc as a pre-rendered image with a full corner radius (a circle), the puck a quad; drag through mouse handlers |
| Curve plot | `canvas()` with `PathBuilder` for the spline (computed by the Rust spline of `ym-color`), quads for points |
| Histogram | `PathBuilder` fills per channel |
| Colour warp plane | the hue field as an image, rings and axes as paths, the scatter as small quads, pins as focusable elements with ids |
| Look grid (~600 tiles) | `gpui-base`'s `v_virtual_list` over rows of tiles; GPUI's own lists are vertical only [G3] |
| Filmstrip | `gpui-base`'s `h_virtual_list` [G3] |
| Thumbnails | images from the core's RGBA (no PNG, no `ym-frame` scheme); an LRU of our own that calls `drop_image` on eviction (GPUI's `image_gallery` example has one); about 105 MB of textures if all 600 look tiles are loaded at 256 × 171 [G2] |
| Crop frame, pick layer, readout, chips | quads, paths and text over the photo |

Scrolling uses AppKit's own deltas, so momentum should feel native; rubber-banding is in `gpui-base`; issue #61637 ("MacOS: Jumpy scrolling") is open [G2, G3, G5]. Scrollbars are not in core GPUI; Zed's are in its GPL `ui` crate, `gpui-component`'s are Apache-2.0 [G2, G3].

### 4.5 Repaint and cost

GPUI draws on demand from a display link: nothing is rebuilt while nothing changes, ProMotion runs at 120 Hz and keeps presenting for a second after fast input so the display does not drop its rate, inactive windows are capped at 30 fps, and frames are capped at 60 under thermal pressure [G2, G9]. Input latency and idle CPU are unmeasured. Issues to watch: #54659 (atlas panic), #61637 (jumpy scrolling), #64050 and #64053 (window chrome glitches), #15166 (whole-window repaints); an opaque-layer change for speed (#44334) was reverted for tearing (#45405) and landed again (#45434) [G5].

## 5. The Mac around it

### 5.1 Window chrome

- **Works today:** `TitlebarOptions { appears_transparent, traffic_light_position }` (the title bar of Tauri's `Overlay`, traffic lights placed again on open and resize, not in full screen), `window_min_size`, `toggle_fullscreen`, `zoom_window` [G2].
- Dragging: `WindowControlArea` does nothing on macOS; with `app_owns_titlebar_drag: true` our header calls `start_window_move()` and, on a double click, `titlebar_double_click()`, which follows the system setting (zoom, fill, minimise or nothing). `gpui-component`'s `TitleBar` does exactly this and is Apache-2.0 [G2, G3].
- An opaque window starts black, so no white flash is expected (unverified) [G2].

### 5.2 Menus and keys

- **Works today:** `cx.set_menus` builds a real `NSMenu`; the first menu is the app menu, one named "Window" becomes the system's Window menu, Services and the Dock menu work; items are enabled through `cx.is_action_available`, so Copy Settings, Paste Settings and Paste Settings to Picked follow the handlers the focused view registers, with no `set_menu_state` [G2].
- ⌘ keys go through GPUI's keymap before the menu, so ⌘Z, ⌘C and ⌘V reach a focused text field first [G2]. The rule of `menu.rs` (keys that also type stay out of the menu) may then relax; G4 checks it.
- **About: with effort.** There is no standard About panel; call `orderFrontStandardAboutPanelWithOptions:` through objc2 with the credits of `about.rs`, or show our own dialog [G2].
- A quirk: the shortcut a menu item shows is chosen against Zed's hard-coded key contexts [G2]; check that ours show.

### 5.3 Quit

`on_app_quit` handlers run as futures within `SHUTDOWN_TIMEOUT`, 200 ms, and `applicationShouldTerminate:` is not implemented in 0.3.9 or on main, so a quit cannot be delayed or cancelled (`gpui-pre-0.3.9/src/app.rs`, lines 79 and 1157 to 1193) [G2]. `Core::shutdown` waits up to 5 s for an export to delete its temporary file (`docs/01`). With effort: our own ⌘Q action asks while an export runs, waits for the shutdown, then calls `cx.quit()`; a quit from the Dock or at logout can still only run the handlers, so the synchronous part of a quit handler would have to call the shutdown itself (blocking the main thread for up to 5 s; unverified that GPUI allows it). Otherwise a partial `.yellowmoon-*.tmp` can be left in an export folder in that case.

### 5.4 Dialogs and shell

- **Works today:** `prompt_for_paths` (files, folders, several), `prompt_for_new_path`, `window.prompt` (a native alert sheet), `open_url`, `reveal_path` (Show in Finder), `open_with_system` [G2]. The open panel has no file-type filter: `Core::open_paths` already ignores non-ARW files, so nothing is lost.
- **In-window dialogs:** `gpui-component`'s `open_dialog` and `open_alert_dialog`, with focus traps [G3].
- **Finder drops: works today.** `drag_over::<ExternalPaths>` gives the hover state, `on_drop` the paths; folders arrive as paths [G2].
- Opening from Finder or the Dock (`on_open_urls`, `on_reopen`) works once `Info.plist` declares document types [G2]; not needed now.

### 5.5 Trackpad and keyboard

- **Two-finger pan:** pixel deltas and the touch phase arrive; AppKit's momentum phase is never read, so momentum events arrive as scrolling that cannot be told from finger movement or seen to end [G2]. For panning the photo that is what we want (the photo coasts as in Preview); for "the gesture ended, draw sharp now" we keep our own timer.
- **Pinch: works today** (`PinchEvent`, `on_pinch`; PRs #47351, #51354) [G2, G5].
- **Smart zoom: missing**; an `NSEvent` monitor through objc2 is the workaround (unverified) [G2].
- **⌥ readout:** modifier-change events exist [G2]. **IME** is strong (Zed's own needs).
- **Tab navigation:** `tab_index`, `tab_stop` and `focus_visible` exist, but the app binds Tab and Shift-Tab to `focus_next` and `focus_prev` itself [G2].

## 6. Controls and accessibility

### 6.1 gpui-component against the C2 controls

| C2 control | gpui-component 0.8.0 [G3] |
|---|---|
| Button (glass, yellow, link) | `Button`, `ButtonGroup`, `DropdownButton` |
| Toggle, ToggleGroup | `Toggle`, `ToggleGroup` (with `.segmented()`) |

### 6.2 Accessibility

- **Shipped, young.** AccessKit landed in PR #56065 (merged 2026-05-27); 0.3.9 uses `accesskit` 0.24 and `accesskit_macos` 0.26, on by default (`Application::new_inaccessible` turns it off). An element is exposed only with an id and a role; the API has `.role()`, `aria_label`, numeric values with minimum, maximum and step, selection, and actions such as increment [G2, G5].
- Zed itself keeps it off unless `ZED_EXPERIMENTAL_A11Y=1`, so few people have run it in anger; open fixes include a crash at window close on Touch Bar Macs with accessibility on (PR #65186) [G2, G5].
- `gpui-component` gives roles to Button, Checkbox, Input, Tab, List, Menu, Alert, Tree and Progress, and none to Slider, Switch, Radio, Select, Dialog, Popover and TitleBar [G3]: our wrappers add them.
- Focus rings come from the theme. `debug_a11y_tree_json()` can feed tests [G2]. VoiceOver itself is a Mac check.

## 9. Risks

1. **The source of the dependency.** Zed does not publish GPUI; one person publishes the weekly snapshot and the component library that pins it. If that stops, `gpui-unofficial` (Zed's release tags) or vendoring the snapshot are the fallbacks; staying on one snapshot is always possible, without fixes.
2. **Weekly, unannounced breaks** in patch versions, pinned with `=`; upgrades are deliberate and cost about half a day each.
3. **Accessibility is new** in GPUI and off in Zed itself; our VoiceOver requirements of `docs/11` lean on code few people have run.
4. **The photo path** is a patched `surface()` we carry until upstream takes it, or the compositing through a hole we have today. The open atlas panic after long sessions with many images (#54659) still concerns the thumbnails and look tiles.
5. **Quit cannot be delayed**, and a Dock or logout quit during an export may leave a temporary file unless the handler can block (section 5.3).
6. **Text without variable weights or letter-spacing**: five static faces, and caps labels without tracking.
7. **The GPL around GPUI.** The examples GPUI points to are mostly in Zed's GPL crates; every agent working on the port must keep to reading them, never copying, and the review must check it.
8. **Size of the tree**: 490 crates, more than twice egui's, and more `cargo deny` and `THIRD-PARTY.md` surface.
9. **"Sluggish" may stay** where it is the render, as for egui.
10. **The one-way update.** Installed copies update into the first GPUI build through their Tauri updater, which checks no macOS version; from then on our own updater carries every update, so both the macOS 14 check and an update from the first GPUI build to the next must pass on your Mac before the first GPUI release.

# Skin node mockups: notes (S-11 design step)

Pictures only. Nothing here is product code. The briefs are `docs/27` §1, §3, §10 (S-11) and §11 (the owner: "own node for this, not available in simple main view"), `docs/23` §7 for how nodes appear, and the research on how other tools present per-face retouching (`research/reports/Skin retouching models and data.md`, the Capture One section; `…/tools_and_evaluation.md`). The look is today's app (C2 dark glass).

## What I drew, and why

- **Where it lives.** Add node › **Skin**, after Film and before the rule, with the icon "scan face" and the line "Retouches the skin of each face." Not in the simple view: screen 02 shows that view with its existing note for photos that have nodes, no Skin controls and no new tab. A photo with a Skin node still shows the retouching everywhere (viewer, export), as with any node.
- **The card.** Numbered like Film (`docs/23` §3.2 says a Film node is "a numbered node like a corrector"; Skin has the same `picture` and `key` ports, masks and Bypass). It takes its place in the flow, so Main is 01 and Skin 02. The only difference is a small face glyph in the footer, where mask glyphs sit, in the quiet label colour (not the mask accent), so it is not mistaken for a corrector (question 5). States: selected (yellow ring), bypassed (half-strength thumbnail and slash), dead (45 %), as for any card.
- **The panel.** Head "02 Skin" with the line "Skin · 5 faces" ("Skin · 1 face", "Skin · no faces found", "Skin · finding faces", "Skin · bypassed"). Two tabs, **Skin** (in the Look tab's yellow, as Film's) and **Mask** (the standard Mask tab of any node; not redrawn, it is `design/film/09-mask-tab.jpg`). Bypass, Reset and More as on a corrector.
- **Skin tab, three cards.**
  1. **Faces.** A round crop of each face, 52 px, with its number; the selected one has a yellow ring and the same ring is drawn round the face on the photo (a soft yellow oval; the others have a dashed white one, each with its number). Clicking a crop or a face on the photo selects it. Five crops fit in a row; with more, the list shows about two and a half rows and scrolls, with the last row fading out. Faces are numbered left to right. The header says "5 found". Under the crops, **Same for all faces** (a switch; hidden with one face).
  2. **Face 2** (or **All faces**, or **Face** with one): the eye toggle (Show masks), Reset, then **Blemish**, **Include neck**, **Dark circles**, **Even skin**, **Texture** (a bipolar slider, indented under Even skin) and **Include neck**. The two neck switches sit under Blemish and Even skin as `docs/27` §3 has them. Sliders are the app's standard row: a changed value gets the yellow field and its own Reset (Face 2's Blemish is 45). With Same for all faces off and several faces, **Copy to other faces** sits under the rows.
  3. **Protect.** Paint · Erase, Size, Clear; the line "Paint over moles, freckles and scars you want to keep." The brush paints on the photo (cursor circle, ⌥ erases, "Paint on the photo. Hold ⌥ to erase." while active) and a stroke is sent on release, as for a Brush row. One protection plane for the whole node, not per face (the spec's `protect` argument is one mask).
- **Defaults.** A new Skin node starts at Blemish 30, Dark circles 30, Even skin 25, Texture 0, neck switches off, the numbers the owner suggested from the research (Capture One suggests starting Blemish between 0 and 40 and reports that moles start to go above 40 to 50; its natural-looking Even skin pair is Amount 40, Texture 50, and its polished one 80 and −70). Reset returns to these, not to 0; off is Bypass. Question 2.
- **Show masks.** The eye in the face card shows, for the selected face, skin (pink, `#ff6fd2`, the mask accent), the under-eye area (blue) and the even-skin core (green dashed outline; the same colour as a fill would blend into the skin tint), with a three-word legend under the rows. With the neck on, the neck is tinted too. It is a view like the mask eye (`docs/23` §5.8): no patch, no undo, ends when you select another node. Question 4.
- **Face too small.** The face stays in the list, dimmed with a dashed ring; selecting it shows "This face is too small to retouch. Faces need to be at least 200 px across in the full photo." and its controls are off. 200 px is Capture One's limit and a placeholder; the research says tune it (open point 5).
- **No faces.** "No faces found in this photo." and what to do: "Skin works on faces that are at least 200 px across and turned towards the camera. Try another photo, or delete this node. It changes nothing until it finds a face." No controls.
- **Finding faces.** A spinner, "Finding faces…", "The first time takes a few seconds. The photo stays as it is until then.", and an indeterminate bar; the controls wait. The header count says "Finding…".
- **Not drawn.** The "Rendering on the CPU" pill (the node renders on the CPU until S-13; the pill is the app's existing one and sits in the header), the Mask tab, tooltips open on the screens (`states.jpg` lists the copy), a Skin node after a mixer, the per-face crops of an even larger list (32), and dark/light variants.

## Questions for the owner (S-H1)

1. **Per face or one setting for all, by default?** Drawn both (03 per face, 06 and the states sheet for all). I propose **Same for all faces on by default** whenever there are several faces: a group photo is usually wanted the same on everyone, and turning it off lets you tune one face; each face then starts from the shared values. Capture One edits faces together or one at a time too. The other reading: per face from the start, with Copy to other faces as the shortcut. Which?
2. **Default amounts and what a new node does.** I suggest Blemish 30, Dark circles 30, Even skin 25, Texture 0 and that a new Skin node **starts retouching at those values at once** (you added it on purpose; Bypass is off). The alternative is every amount at 0 and the person raises them. Reset goes back to the defaults, not to 0. Right?
3. **Where does the protect brush live?** Drawn as its own **Protect** card on the Skin tab, one plane for the node. The alternative is a Brush row in the Mask tab. A mask row says "where the node acts" and Subtract cuts a part out, which is close but not the same. `docs/27` §6.2 applies the protection to Blemish only, so today it does not stop Dark circles or Even skin from touching a protected mole. Say which tools Protect should cover; the card's line "Blemish leaves painted areas alone" is drawn for Blemish only. Also: does Reset of the node clear the protection (I drew the tooltip "Masks and protection stay")?
4. **Is Show masks needed?** It helps people see why a thing changed (and checks the face parts the models find), and I'd keep it. But the toolbar's **Show mask** already exists for a node's mask rows, and a Skin node has both: two "mask" views with two meanings. Options: keep the eye in the face card (drawn); drop it for version 1 and rely on the Before view; or fold it into the toolbar's Show mask when the Skin tab is open. I prefer the first, with the toolbar's Show mask keeping its meaning.
5. **Card number and glyph.** Numbered like Film, with a face glyph in the card's footer. Or no glyph (as Film has none; the name says it). Or no number (a "kind" without a place in the flow): not recommended, since Skin is a normal link in the line.
6. **Mask tab on a Skin node.** I kept it (as Film has) so the retouching can be limited to a part of the photo. Say if it is not wanted; the node would then have one tab.
7. **Smallest face and the cap.** Drawn with 200 px across and a list that scrolls to 32 (Capture One's numbers). The research suggests tuning on public portraits; the copy has "200 px" in it, so a change touches text.
8. **Order of the faces.** Left to right. Alternative: largest first (the people the photo is about come first). Largest first would stop the numbers following the picture.
9. **Copy to other faces** is only in the per-face reading; with Same for all faces on it is hidden. Fine?
10. **The simple view.** Drawn with no Skin controls at all and the existing note. If you want a one-line hint there ("Skin retouching is in the nodes."), that is one more string.
11. **Face crops.** The round crops show the faces as the photo has them (before retouching, from the Input), not after. Keeps the list stable while you slide. Say if you want them to follow the retouching.

## Found while drawing

1. `docs/27` §3 has no word for the two neck switches in the interface; "Include neck" is Capture One's wording and is drawn as is.
2. `docs/27` §6.2: Protect covers Blemish only, and an eye-lift or evening that crosses a protected mole is not stopped. The card's line is therefore Blemish-only (question 3).
3. The face list needs a stable order and a stable id per face across re-finding; the cache key of the maps already uses the face rectangles (docs/27 §6.1).
4. A group of many small faces (06) makes the on-photo numbers small; at Fit 28 % they are readable, and the ring and number scale with the face.

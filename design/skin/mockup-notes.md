# Face Refinement node mockups: notes (S-11 design step)

Pictures only. Nothing here is product code. The briefs are `docs/27` §1, §3, §5.6, §7.7 to §7.9, §10 (S-11, S-15) and §11 (the owner: "own node for this, not available in simple main view"), `docs/23` §7 for how nodes appear, and the research on how other tools present per-face retouching (`research/reports/Skin retouching models and data.md`, the Capture One section; `…/tools_and_evaluation.md`). The look is today's app (C2 dark glass).

## The owner's decisions on these mockups (2026-11-11)

1. **"Don't do include neck."** Both "Include neck" switches are gone from the panel, the states sheet, the legend and the copy; the skin is the face's skin only (spec §3).
2. **"Let's add some things from Resolve: teeth whitening, skin grading (contrast, shadow desaturation, shine removal)."** Added as a **Skin grading** card (Contrast, Shadow desaturation, Shine removal) and a **Teeth** card (Whitening), per face, each with its own Reset. The teeth area is its own tint in Show skin areas.
3. **"Node name should be Face Refinement, since it is about faces and not generally skin."** The node is **Face Refinement** (kind `face_refinement`) everywhere: Add node item, card title, panel head. The panel's first tab is **Face** (the second stays **Mask**). Folder and file names keep "skin" (`design/skin/`, `03-skin-per-face.jpg`); a rename is a `git mv` if wanted.

## What I drew, and why

- **Where it lives.** Add node › **Face Refinement**, after Film and before the rule, with the "scan face" icon and the line "Refines each face: skin, under-eyes and teeth." Not in the simple view: screen 02 shows that view with its existing note for photos that have nodes, no Face Refinement controls and no new tab. A photo with the node still shows the retouching everywhere (viewer, export), as with any node.
- **The card.** Numbered like Film (`docs/23` §3.2: a numbered node like a corrector; `picture` and `key` ports, masks, Bypass). It takes its place in the flow: Main is 01, Face Refinement 02. A small face glyph sits in the footer where mask glyphs sit, in the quiet label colour, so it is not mistaken for a corrector (question 5). States: selected (yellow ring), bypassed (half-strength thumbnail and slash), dead (45 %). **The card label reads "Face Refi…"**: the app shortens any name over 10 letters on the card (the rule in the captured graph); the panel head shows the full name (see "Found while drawing").
- **The panel.** Head "02 Face Refinement" and the line "5 faces" ("1 face", "No faces found", "Finding faces"). The name is shown in full in the head; to make room, the line after it is the part that truncates (a one-rule change, `skin.css`). Two tabs: **Face** (in the Look tab's yellow, as Film's first tab) and **Mask** (the standard Mask tab; not redrawn, it is `design/film/09-mask-tab.jpg`). Bypass, Reset and More as on a corrector.
- **Face tab, five cards, in this order:** **Faces**, **Face N** (or **All faces**, or **Face** with one), **Skin grading**, **Teeth**, **Protect** (the order of the brief: Faces, the per-face controls, Protect).
  1. **Faces.** A round crop of each face, 52 px, with its number; the selected one has a yellow ring and the same ring is drawn round the face on the photo (a soft yellow oval; the others have a dashed white one, each with its number). Clicking a crop or a face on the photo selects it. Five crops fit in a row; with more, the list shows about two and a half rows and scrolls, with the last row fading out. Faces are numbered left to right. The header says "5 found". Under the crops, **Same for all faces** (a switch; hidden with one face).
  2. **Face 2** (title follows the selection): the eye toggle (**Show skin areas**), Reset, then **Blemish**, **Dark circles**, **Even skin** and **Texture** (a bipolar slider, indented under Even skin). A changed value gets the yellow field and its own Reset (Face 2's Blemish is 45).
  3. **Skin grading:** **Contrast** (bipolar, −100 to 100, centre 0), **Shadow desaturation** (0 to 100), **Shine removal** (0 to 100). Reset sets all three to 0.
  4. **Teeth:** **Whitening** (0 to 100). Reset sets it to 0. With Same for all faces off and several faces, **Copy to other faces** sits at the foot of this card, the last of the per-face cards, with the line "Copies all of Face 2's settings." (it copies the three cards).
  5. **Protect.** Paint · Erase, Size, Clear; the line "Paint over moles, freckles and scars you want to keep." The brush paints on the photo (cursor circle, ⌥ erases, "Paint on the photo. Hold ⌥ to erase." while active) and a stroke is sent on release, as for a Brush row. One protection plane for the node.
- **Why separate cards, not one long Face card.** One card with eleven rows and two sub-groups would read as a wall, and each group wants its own Reset (the brief). The cards in the app's panel are the unit of "a group with a Reset", so the grading and the teeth are cards of their own; the two later cards carry a small tag ("Face 2" / "All faces") on the right of the title so you can tell whom they act on after scrolling. The eye stays in the Face card. The cost is length: the panel no longer fits one screen (5 cards, about 1.4 screens). 03, 04 and 06 show it from the top (Protect out of view); 05 shows it scrolled to its end; the states sheet shows every panel full length.
- **Defaults.** A new node starts at Blemish 30, Dark circles 30, Even skin 25, Texture 0 (still open, question 2) and **Contrast 0, Shadow desaturation 0, Shine removal 0, Whitening 0**, so a new node does what the earlier three did and nothing more. Reset (the head and each card) returns to these, not to all 0; off is Bypass.
- **Show skin areas.** The eye in the Face card shows, for the selected face: skin (pink, `#ff6fd2`, the mask accent; the mouth's inside is cut out), under the eyes (blue), **teeth (violet, its own tint)** and the even-skin core (green dashed outline, which is also where Skin grading acts, spec §5.6; legend "Even skin and grading"). It is a view like the mask eye (`docs/23` §5.8): no patch, no undo, it ends when you select another node. A face with no open mouth shows no violet tint. Question 4.
- **The drawings now have something to point at.** `faces.py` draws an open smile with yellowish teeth on some faces (family 1 and 2, the portrait, group faces 1, 6 and 9), a little sheen on the forehead, nose and cheek (family 2, 3 and 4, the portrait, group faces 4 and 9), and a slightly warm green shadow on one side of a face (family 4, the portrait, group face 6), so Whitening, Shine removal and Shadow desaturation each have a face to be tried on. They are drawings: the real result is judged on photographs (S-H2).
- **Face too small.** The face stays in the list, dimmed with a dashed ring; selecting it shows "This face is too small to retouch. Faces need to be at least 200 px across in the full photo." and all its controls (the three cards) are off. 200 px is Capture One's limit and a placeholder.
- **No faces.** "No faces found in this photo." and what to do: "Face Refinement works on faces that are at least 200 px across and turned towards the camera. Try another photo, or delete this node. It changes nothing until it finds a face." No controls.
- **Finding faces.** A spinner, "Finding faces…", "The first time takes a few seconds. The photo stays as it is until then.", an indeterminate bar; the controls wait.
- **Not drawn.** The "Rendering on the CPU" pill, the Mask tab, tooltips open on the screens (`states.jpg` lists the copy), the node after a mixer, a list of 32 faces, a face whose mouth is closed (Whitening then does nothing; the copy has a line for it), dark/light variants.

## Questions for the owner (S-H1)

1. **Per face or one setting for all, by default?** Drawn both (03 per face; 06 and the states sheet for all). I propose **Same for all faces on by default** whenever there are several faces: a group photo is usually wanted the same on everyone; turning it off lets you tune one face. The other reading: per face from the start, with Copy to other faces as the shortcut. Which?
2. **Default amounts and what a new node does.** I suggest Blemish 30, Dark circles 30, Even skin 25, Texture 0, the four new amounts 0, and that a new node **starts retouching at those values at once**. The alternative is every amount at 0. Reset goes back to the defaults, not to 0. Right?
3. **Where does the protect brush live, and what does it cover?** Drawn as its own **Protect** card, one plane for the node; the alternative is a Brush row in the Mask tab. `docs/27` §6.2 applies Protect to Blemish only, so a protected mole can still be changed by Dark circles, Even skin and the grading. Say which tools Protect should cover (all of them, including grading and whitening?); the card's line "Blemish leaves painted areas alone" is drawn for Blemish only. Does Reset of the node clear the protection (tooltip drawn: "Masks and protection stay")?
4. **The eye toggle and its name.** Drawn as **Show skin areas** in the Face card (it shows skin, under the eyes, teeth and the grading core). The toolbar's **Show mask** already exists for mask rows, so there are two views with two meanings. Options: keep the eye (drawn); drop it for version 1 and use Before; or fold it into the toolbar's Show mask when the Face tab is open. I prefer the first. Is "Show skin areas" the right name, given that it also tints teeth?
5. **Card number and glyph.** Numbered like Film, with a face glyph in the footer. Or no glyph; or no number (not recommended).
6. **Mask tab on this node.** Kept (as Film has) so the retouching can be limited to a part of the photo. Say if it is not wanted.
7. **Smallest face and the cap.** 200 px across and a list that scrolls to 32 (Capture One's numbers); the copy has "200 px" in it.
8. **Order of the faces.** Left to right. Alternative: largest first.
9. **Copy to other faces** only in the per-face reading; hidden with Same for all faces on. It now copies the Skin grading and Teeth too. Fine?
10. **The simple view.** No controls and the existing note. A one-line hint ("Face Refinement is in the nodes.") is one more string.
11. **Face crops.** Show the faces as the Input has them, not after retouching.
12. **New: should Skin grading and Teeth be per face, or always for all faces?** Drawn per face, like the other cards, because a face in shadow or a face with a yellow cast may need its own amount, and the model (`docs/27` §3) has them per face. The alternative is one setting for all faces whatever Same for all faces says (Resolve's Face Refinement has one set of controls per face too). Which?
13. **New: one card or three?** Drawn as three (Face, Skin grading, Teeth) for a Reset each and shorter groups; the panel then scrolls. The alternative is the grading and teeth rows inside the one Face card with a sub-heading each, which reads shorter at a glance but has one Reset for eleven rows.
14. **New: the card name "Face" for the first tab, and "Face N" for the first card.** Both say "Face"; the card changes with the selection. Alternative: name the first card "Skin" ("Skin · Face 2") and keep "Face" for the tab. Fine as drawn?
15. **New: the card label "Face Refi…".** The app's 10-letter rule cuts the node name on the card (and the Add node, panel and graph all use the same name). Either widen the card label for this node, or let the rule allow about 15 letters (a change in the graph code, not in this design), or accept it.
16. **New: Whitening with no teeth.** The node does nothing and the controls stay on (spec §5.6: a missing mouth only turns whitening off). Should the Teeth card say so ("No teeth to whiten on this face.") only when the slider is above 0, or always when none are found? Drawn in copy only.

## Found while drawing

1. `docs/27` §3 gave no interface words for the two neck switches; now moot.
2. `docs/27` §6.2: Protect covers Blemish only; the grading, whitening and eye work are not stopped by a protected mole (question 3).
3. The face list needs a stable order and a stable id per face across re-finding; the cache key of the maps already uses the face rectangles (docs/27 §6.1).
4. A group of many small faces (06) makes the on-photo numbers small; at Fit 28 % they are readable.
5. The panel head's name has a maximum width of 58 % of the text area in the captured CSS; "Face Refinement" is cut with it ("Face Refinem…"). The mockup lifts that limit for the name and lets the line after it truncate. The interface task must do the same, or the name is cut.
6. The label column of the slider row is 92 px; "Shadow desaturation" needs about 132 px. The mockup widens the column to 132 px (118 px for the indented Texture), which shortens the slider track by about 40 px. In the narrower sheet panels the label still wraps to two lines.
7. The Skin grading and Teeth cards are tagged "Face 2" so that the selection is clear after scrolling; the faces card is far above them.

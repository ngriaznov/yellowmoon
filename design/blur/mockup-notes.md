# Blur mockups: notes (BL-01, 2026-11-18)

What the drawings decided where `docs/33` left room. The questions for the owner are in `docs/33` §10.

1. **One node, one Type control.** The four types are a glasscn segmented control (Soft, Lens, Motion, Zoom) at the top of the Blur card, full width. The controls below change with the type; each type's values are kept when the type changes. A select would hide the choice that most changes the panel.
2. **The mask switch is its own card.** "Respect mask edges" is a glasscn switch in a card called **Mask edges**, under the Blur card, on by default, with a two-line explanation that changes with the switch. With no mask it says it changes nothing yet. It is one switch for all four types.
3. **The halo is shown, not described.** `09` puts the same picture, mask and blur side by side with the switch off and on, with the jacket's edge three times larger. The number printed on it (27 % of the blurred colour comes from the figure just outside the silhouette with the switch off; 0 % on) is measured on the scene by the sketch.
4. **Lens shows why Highlights exists.** The lights are far above white in linear light; the hexagons are the blades. Highlights 60 weights them so they read as clear discs.
5. **Rotation is off with Round**, and Highlights 0 is the plain aperture (`states.jpg`).
6. **Zoom's centre is a handle on the photo** with eight dashed rays, a Pick centre toggle in the card and the line "Click the photo or drag the handle.", as the Radial mask's centre is. The centre stays sharp.
7. **A new node blurs at once**: Radius 20 (`states.jpg`, "A new node"). The alternative, Radius 0, is a question for the owner.
8. **Head and card.** The name is "Blur"; the head's line is the type ("Soft", "Lens"), "Bypassed" or "Not connected". The card's glyph is three concentric dotted rings beside the mask glyph. The tab is "Blur", in the Detail tab's colour (a soft peach), not Colour Uniform's green or Film's yellow.
9. **Show mask is the ordinary one** (`06`): no second view, no eye, no change with the type.
10. **The simple view has no controls** (`08`), like every node but Main.
11. **The scene is synthetic**, so no photograph of the owner's is used. The figure's face is a flat drawing.

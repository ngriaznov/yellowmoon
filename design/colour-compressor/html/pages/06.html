Colour Compressor · how the pull works

Drawn from the maths of docs/32 §3 by the sketch compress_ref.py, not by hand. Each colour keeps the share 1 − κW of its distance to the target on each axis: 0 % leaves it, 100 % puts it on the target. Outside the range W is 0, so nothing moves; at the soft edge it moves by a share.

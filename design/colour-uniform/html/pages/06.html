Colour Uniform · how the pull works

Drawn from the maths of docs/32 §3 by the sketch compress_ref.py, not by hand. On each axis a colour keeps the share 1 − κ of its distance to the target (with a neutral guard on hue and saturation, and a fade on the far side of the wheel for hue): 0 % leaves it, 100 % puts it on the target. The node has no range of its own: its masks decide which pixels it changes.

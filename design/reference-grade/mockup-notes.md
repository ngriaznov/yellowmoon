# Reference Grade mockups: notes (RG-07, 2026-11-25)

What the drawings decided where `docs/34` left room, then what they showed to be awkward. The values in every picture are illustrative; no fit was run.

## What the drawings decided

1. **The Reference card is one card, in the order of §9.2.** Thumbnail with its name under it, then one row with Choose reference… and Fit, Strength with its line, Skin protection with its line, and the status block (status line, then notes, then an error if there is one). A line above the status, a hairline, separates the status from the controls so it reads as a result.
2. **The thumbnail is a letterboxed box, 128 px high** (the whole reference, never cropped), with the file name under it ("harbour-dusk.jpg", or "Photo 3 of 7" for a strip photo). With no reference it is a chequered placeholder with an image glyph and the words "No reference". Nothing about the reference but its name is shown (§3.7).
3. **Fit has three looks.** *Fit* is the prominent button (the yellow of Export) once there is a reference; it is off, and says why in a tooltip ("Choose a reference first."), without one. After a fit, *Fit again* is an ordinary glass button, so it does not shout. When the fit is stale (the look changed), *Fit again* is the prominent button with a ring and a glow: that is the accent of §2.2. "Edited by hand" does not use the accent (the spec gives it to the stale look only).
4. **While fitting**, Choose reference… is off, the progress bar and Cancel take the Fit row, and the step is the status line ("Finding what is in the pictures…", "Fitting… 1 of 3" to "3 of 3"). The step is therefore not said twice. The picture does not change until the fit is done; a cancel changes nothing.
5. **Strength is on from the start**, at 100 %, with no reference too (it scales nothing yet and does no harm). Its Reset appears when it is below 100. The line under it is §9.2's.
6. **The status text is the spec's, not the brief's.** The brief for RG-07 said "Matched to the reference."; §9.3 says **"Fitted to the reference."** and the drawings use the spec's. Every other status line, note and error is copied from §9.3 without change (checked by eye against the file; `11-status-and-errors.jpg`). Two small marks the spec does not have: a note carries an info glyph; an error is a red-tinted notice with an alert glyph and, as the spec says, no code.
7. **"Too small" keeps the *N*.** `docs/34` §16.6 has not set *N*, so the drawing prints an italic *N*, not a number.
8. **Choose reference… is a menu button** with a chevron and two items, **Image file…** (the system open dialog, which is the system's and is not drawn) and **Photo in the strip**. While the strip chooser is on, the button reads as pressed in the Reference tab's yellow, as Pick colour does.
9. **The strip chooser** (§9.2, §6.4) replaces the graph in the lower part of the node view with the filmstrip (the view's toolbar is dimmed and inert), and a pill over the photo says "Click a photo in the strip. Esc cancels." The photo being graded carries a "This photo" badge and is off; a photo still developing carries a "Developing" badge and is off, with the tooltip "This photo isn't ready yet." The tooltip of the photo being graded is "That's the photo you're grading. Choose another." The tiles are 156 × 104 px.
10. **Seven tabs fit at 400 px** (Reference, Light, Colour, Curve, Hue, Elements, Mask) at **12 px**, one point below the other nodes' 13 px, with no label shortened or clipped (`shoot.mjs` checks for clipped text on every page and reports none). §9.1 expected the row to clip and asked RG-12 to test it; at 12 px with 2 px side padding it does not clip at 1440 × 900. RG-12 still tests 1180 × 760 and 1800 × 1100.
11. **Tab colours.** Reference is in the Look yellow, Light in the Light blue, Colour, Hue and Elements in the Colour green, Curve in the Curve orange, Mask in the mask accent. The head reads "Match" (the node's name) with the line "Reference Grade"; the card on the graph reads "Match".
12. **Hue vs hue** is drawn on its full axis (−180° to +180°, a dashed detent at 0), **Hue vs saturation on a log₂ scale** from ⅛ to 8 with its detent at 1 (the "0" label sits on the bottom edge: a multiplier of 0 is grey and cannot be on a log axis, so the axis starts at ⅛ and the label means "down to grey"). The log scale is the choice §9.4 left to RG-07: a linear 0 to 8 puts 0.85 to 1.15 within a pixel of the line. The x axis is the hue, 0° to 360°, with the rainbow track of Colour Uniform's Hue slider under the plot. The fit's six knots are drawn; the selected point is filled and the card says what it is ("Hue 120°, Turn +12°", "Hue 120°, Saturation 85%"). Each card has its own Reset.
13. **Elements cards** (§9.4a): a card per element in §2.3's order (Sky, Vegetation, Water, Ground, Built, Skin, Subject), each with the header "Sky", the element's share of the photo ("26 % of the photo"), an eye, a Reset when changed, and the rows **Exposure (EV)** (−1 to +1, default 0), **Hue** (± the element's cap, in degrees) and **Chroma** (50 to 160 %, default 100 %, the fill running from 100 %). The Subject has Exposure only.
14. **Show mask per element: yes, as an eye in each card header** (the spec left it to RG-07). It shows that element's soft mask `a_e` in the mask accent over the photo (`06c-element-mask.jpg`, the Sky), like the eye of Face Refinement's cards and the Mask tab's rows. One at a time. The node's own Show mask button is not used by it (that one shows the node's own mask rows). The Subject has an eye too (its mask is the person). An element not found has an eye that is off.
15. **An element not found is greyed, not hidden.** Under 2 % of the photo (§9.4a), the card keeps its place and its sliders (they still work: a grade there acts on nothing), says **"Not found in this photo."** under the header, dims its title and rows, and its eye is off. In the example the pond is 0.6 % of the picture, so **Water is left at the identity by the fit** and the picture shows no water grade. Skin is found (3.3 %); the sheet draws the rest.
16. **"Looking at the photo…"** replaces the share in every card's header, with a small spinner, while the scene is being made; the eye is off and the sliders still work (`06b`, the last cell). It is the same simplest reading as the absent card.
17. **The look-changed picture** shows the Output's look changed (the corner label reads "NEUTRAL · 100%" where the others read "SIGNATURE · 100%") and the picture a little different, so the state is not drawn out of nothing. No number in the card moved.
18. **Edited by hand** is shown on the Reference tab with the picture of a hand edit (Vegetation Hue +22°, Chroma 75 %).
19. **Add node**: Reference Grade is the last of the nodes, **after Blur**, before the separator, with the line "Matches the grade of a reference picture." (see "Awkward" 3).
20. **The glyph (the spec left it to RG-07).** A picture beside a picture, with a grade curve in the front one: a rounded square with an S-shaped curve in it, and the corner of a second frame behind it. It is drawn with the same 2 px stroke and round caps as Blur's and Colour Uniform's glyphs, sits beside the mask glyph in the card's footer at 14 px, and in the Add node menu at 24 px. `states.jpg` shows it at 14, 24, 48 and 96 px. Alternatives I drew and dropped: two overlapping squares (too like Layer's glyph), a dropper (a pick tool's, and Colour Uniform's pick already is one).
21. **The pictures.** The photo is a village with a figure; the reference is a different picture (a harbour at dusk), so the sheet shows that a reference is matched in its distributions, not pixel by pixel. The illustrative fit lifts the sky a little and turns it a little towards teal, makes the foliage a darker teal-green and less saturated, warms the ground and the walls towards amber, and warms the skin slightly. `12-before-after.jpg` shows the photo, the reference, the fit, the fit at 50 % and the fit without the element grades, and prints the values.
22. **The maths of the sketch follows the spec except one step.** `rg_ref.py` restores the Rec.709 luma after the hue step, as the owner answered for STATUS question 48 (2026-11-25), where `docs/34` §4.2 still says the step keeps the mean and does not restore luma. §4.2 and §15 decision 19 need to follow the owner's answer. The elements do not restore luma (§4a.2). Strength is `ReferenceGradeNode::effective` of §3.2 (curves knotwise, a ratio as a power). The soft masks are §4a.3's guided filter (radius 0.02 of the short side, ε 0.001, L*/100 as the guide) on the drawing's own layers, so a mask is as soft as the product's, not the segmenter's.

## What the drawings showed to be awkward in `docs/34`

1. **§9.2 and `docs/23`: the strip is hidden in the node view, and the Reference tab lives there.** `docs/23` hides the filmstrip in the node view and replaces it with the switcher "‹ 2 of 7 ›", and says the graph is inert while Crop or Export shows. §9.2 says "the filmstrip becomes a chooser". The drawing takes the simplest reading: while the chooser is on, the strip appears in the graph's place and the graph is out of sight. If the owner wants the graph kept, the strip would have to go over it as a bar. A decision for RG-H2.
2. **§9.4: a hue move of a fit is small on the spec's axes.** A fitted `hue_vs_hue` of a few degrees on a ±180° axis, and a `hue_vs_sat` of 0.85 to 1.15 on a 0 to 8 axis, are within about 5 px of the detent line on a 340 px wide plot: the curves look flat and the points can hardly be told apart (`05-hue-tab.jpg` is honest about it). The ranges are the spec's (±180°, 0 to 8) and the drawing keeps them. Suggest for RG-H2: the plot shows ±45° and ×⅓ to ×3 by default and a "Zoom out" to the full range, or a second, finer scale, with the number field and the keyboard still reaching ±180° and 8. Not drawn, because it is a change to §9.4.
3. **The Add node order.** §2.1 says the Reference Grade item is "after Colour Uniform", and `docs/33` (Blur, built after) says the same for Blur. Both cannot be second. The drawing puts Blur after Colour Uniform and Reference Grade after Blur (the order the nodes were specified in). One of the two specs should say "after the last node".
4. **§4.2 against STATUS question 48** (luma), above, item 22.
5. **The card title "Match" and the head line "Reference Grade"** are both short and clear in the head; but the tab that opens first is also called "Reference" and the card in it is called "Reference". Three words alike in one place. They are the spec's and the drawing keeps them; the card title could be dropped (a card with no header, only the thumbnail) if the owner thinks it is too much.
6. **"The same photo" has two homes.** §6.4 and §9.3 have the strip tile off with a reason and, for `ym` and a file, an error "That's the photo you're grading. Choose another." The card's error is drawn (`11`), though in the app the strip never lets it happen; it can only come from a file that is the photo. Not a problem, a note for RG-12 to decide whether the error needs a test.
7. **The "values, no reference" state** ("The values are kept. Choose a reference to fit again.") has a Fit that is off with the tooltip "Choose a reference first." That is right, but the person has values and no way to re-fit but to choose; the line says so. No change.
8. **A reference picture is shown with black bars removed or not?** The thumbnail shows the picture as it was read, not as it was reduced; the note "Black bars were removed from the reference." is the only sign. The simplest reading; the drawings do not show the reduced picture.

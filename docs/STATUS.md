# Status

The current state of Yellowmoon: release, milestone, what is built, gate results, open items and the environment the agent works in. Specs in `docs/` hold the technical detail; this page summarises and links.

## Project

- **Repository:** github.com/ngriaznov/yellowmoon, on the owner's personal account like plugout.
- **Domain:** not given yet. `docs/12-distribution.md` and the app have no domain-dependent setting.
- **Licence:** Apache-2.0 (`docs/14-licensing.md`).
- **What it is:** a free, open-source macOS raw processor for Sony ARW files. It renders each photo the way an ARRI ALEXA (ALEV III sensor) would, through spectral sensor emulation, a LogC4 / ACES 2 colour pipeline and small learned models, with very few controls. Rust crates under `crates/` hold all image processing; a Tauri + React app and the `ym` CLI are the two front ends.
- **Workflow:** work is on `main`; a version bump on `main` publishes a release (`docs/12-distribution.md`).

**GPU path.** `Auto` uses hardware adapters only and the CPU for software adapters (lavapipe, WARP). A device error re-renders the whole photo on the CPU, never a mix of tiles, and golden hashes pin the CPU reference. On the device: the raw front (R1 to R5b, R9, sigma plane, fit-view group), R6 to R8, the sampler, the colour chain with looks, output sharpening with C13, magnified views above 100 % and the export tail in slabs of at most 128 MiB. Metal compiles with fast-math, so kernels that must match the CPU pin every product and sum behind an OR barrier (`docs/17-gpu.md` section 13).

*Not on the device yet.* The CPU keeps decode and the preview estimates, the R6 test-switch methods (Menon, LMMSE, bilinear, log-guided), Lossless M/S, histogram counts, the DNG white point, the full-chain R5 offsets and every encoder (`docs/17-gpu.md` section 11); a device JPEG front end would be revisited only if a Mac shows DCT and Huffman coding above about 2 s per 50 MP. The neighbour raw prefetch (`Renderer::spawn_raw_prefetch`) exists but the app does not call it, and the device warm-up is one job a viewport job cannot pass. If the viewport's counters show contention on a Mac, the follow-ups are presenting on its own thread, `AutoNoVsync` or `Mailbox` present modes, pan and zoom applied without waiting for the core, a `viewport_input_batch` command, and `QOS_CLASS_UTILITY` for the decode, thumbnail and export threads. An opt-in upstream option to switch Metal's fast-math off would remove the kernel workarounds.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Sensor-map gate with estimated lights (`docs/02`, M1-06).** As worded it cannot pass: 1° of white error is 1.2 to 1.9 ΔE00 and the gate needs about 0.3°. `docs/02` proposes gating the estimator by angular error only and stating the estimated-light check as the map-only increase (≤ +0.2 median, ≤ +0.7 p95; measured +0.15 and +0.69, so post-hoc). Accept?
2. **GPU storage precision (`docs/17-gpu.md` section 2).** f16 can exceed the 1e-3 spike gate (up to 6e-3) and the 1-code display-transform gate on 3 to 4 of 898 extreme vectors. As built, device storage is f32, which doubles plane memory (50 MP spike peak 0.28 → 0.56 GiB, gate 1.5 GiB). Confirm?
3. **GPU renders and `PIPELINE_VERSION`.** A GPU render may differ from the CPU reference within the stated tolerances; the version does not change and the device is recorded in no file (AGENTS rule 8 read as "same machine and device"). A tag per device would break app = CLI bytes across machines. Confirm?
4. **Memory budget on Apple silicon.** GPU `clamp(RAM/4, 512 MiB, 4 GiB)`, CPU `min(its budget, 0.6 × RAM − GPU)` (8 GB: 2 and 2.8 GiB; 16 GB: 4 and 5.6 GiB). Acceptable on 8 GB Macs?
5. **Software adapters, graphics-card preference, CPU-only list.** (a) `Auto` uses the CPU when the only adapter is a software one. (b) `ym render --json` has additive `device` and `notes` fields; there is no "Use graphics card" preference (the persistence list in `docs/01-architecture.md` is exhaustive; development uses `YELLOWMOON_GPU`). (c) The CPU-only list of `docs/17-gpu.md` section 11 stays for now. Agreed?
6. **The export target.** Is the 6 s export target JPEG-only (`docs/05`: "Full neural export, JPEG")? TIFF and PNG 16-bit encoding alone take about twice that on the dev box.
7. **16-bit TIFF default.** On a real 24 MP frame 16-bit LZW was 26 % larger than uncompressed and 38 % larger than ZIP. Switch the default to ZIP?
8. **Spec error in M1-07.** `docs/15-milestones.md` says "3200 K under daylight renders warmer than Auto"; physically it renders bluer, and the test checks the ALEXA-maths amount with the correct sign. Fix the wording?
9. **Spectral and illuminant tolerances.** (a) M1-02: off grid the 10 K tables reach 5e-6 (blackbody) and 4e-4 (daylight, rounded D-series coefficients) against the 1e-6 spec; accept, or use a 1 K blackbody grid and unrounded daylight? (b) A two-component as-shot prior that discounts a wrong white-balance preset improves p90 4.93 → 4.16° but raises the worst case 22 → 33°; adopt (needs a retrain)? (c) The blackbody and daylight locus branches meet with a 0.04 log-chroma gap, so small magenta tints near 4100 K read with the wrong sign; use one Planckian readout locus or a blend over 3500 to 4500 K (changes `profile.json`)?
10. **IJG licence.** `jpeg-encoder`'s forward DCT is IJG-licensed (permissive; needs the Independent JPEG Group sentence in `NOTICE`, docs and About). It is allowed in `deny.toml`; please confirm.
11. **DNG brightness outside Adobe apps.** Adobe's renderer applies a highlight shoulder rather than a scale for a negative `BaselineExposure`, so the fitted profile stores scene values up to 16 with `BaselineExposure` at the display placement (+0.85). Adobe apps match our JPEG; RawTherapee and darktable show the stored image about 4 EV darker. Recommended: keep the floor and say so in the export help. OK?
12. **Raw-algorithm constants.** Public sources do not explain some constants of the raw-algorithms package (spec 01: 1.6, 2.5, 18 and 102, 121 and 123, 0.03, 0.75, 5, 3; spec 02: the log2 and exp2 cubics; specs 03 and 04: the constants paragraphs of sections 3.3 and 3.4), and spec 01 section 8 reads as if an integer variant exists elsewhere (`research/raw-algorithms-prior-art.md`). Where do they come from (tuned, fitted, taken from another program)? The answer goes into `docs/14-licensing.md`; patent leads go to M4-H4.
13. **Lateral CA on the mosaic (R5b, `docs/05` R9).** R9 after the demosaic made the red/blue shift larger on real Sony files (a green-guided demosaic keeps only about half of the CA). R5b corrects on the mosaic at full resolution, leaves less misregistration on every real file measured, and changes every real-file render with a CA table. Recommended: on by default, as built; the alternative is `decode.chromatic_aberration` off by default. A blind look at a few photos is the natural check.
14. **NEX-5N and NEX-7 lens data.** Their lens tables are only in the encrypted SR2 sub-IFD, which `ym-raw` does not read, so they show "Lens data: none". Reading them is a few lines and switches all three corrections on for NEX files. Read them?
15. **One C++ file in the repository.** `tools/dng/dng_jxl_stub.cpp` (40 lines) is a stand-in that CI compiles with Adobe's DNG SDK to build `dng_validate`. Keep it, or have the CI script write it at build time so the repository holds no C++?
16. **DNG gate against a sharpened JPEG (`docs/09`).** A DNG carries no sharpening while our JPEG does, so on a real file with fine detail the number is mostly sharpening: NEX-5N measures 1.724 (the other eight real files 0.391 to 0.831). Options: (1) keep the gate; (2) compare with a JPEG made with capture and output sharpening off, at the same limit (the DNG checks already measure it this way, median 0.04 to 0.08); (3) raise the limit. Recommended: (2).
17. **White reading for a daylight photo (`docs/03`, White scale of stand-in bodies).** The A1's scale comes from the presets the camera writes into its own files (Daylight preset at 5212 K); the published colour matrix would give 4720 K and misses the limits. The presets read about 30 mired warmer than the published curves on both measured bodies that have both, so the same daylight reads about 500 K apart between an A1 and a measured body. Options: (1) keep as built; (2) apply the presets' scale to the measured bodies too (changes 11 profiles); (3) take the A1's scale from the matrix. Recommended: (1) until a daylight file of a measured body from the CC0 corpus settles it.
18. **More bodies with a white scale.** Local files of seven other bodies (A7 II, A6300, A5000, A300, A500, NEX-5N, NEX-7) give fitted scales, but they are local evaluation files, so nothing is shipped. Add them as registry entries with the file names as provenance (`docs/14-licensing.md` rule 8), or wait for CC0 files? The A1 II uses the A1's scale on the strength of the same published matrix.
19. **ARRI Look Files: Look Intensity and the CDL (`docs/08-looks.md`, Open (ARRI Look Files)).** As built the Intensity (parameter 10006) is not read (a look opens at 100 %) and the CDL ids are inferred from the table order (slope, offset, power, then saturation); both are right for every file we have. Keep it so? A single `.alf4` saved from ARRI's Reference Tool with distinct CDL values (slope 1.1 1.2 1.3, offset 0.01 0.02 0.03, power 0.9 1.1 1.3, saturation 1.5) would settle the ids.
20. **Copy for look files.** The wizard recognises ARRI's two Look Files downloads, but its Choose and Get-the-files screens (text in the mockups and their screenshots), the "Import .cube…" menu item and the Look tab button still talk about 3D-LUT downloads and `.cube` only; changing them means changing the mockups and the baselines. Change the copy (for example "Import LUTs and look files…")?

## Known issues

- **High-ISO colour grain in mid-dark colours (pipeline 0.5.1).** The fix of 0.5.1 (Cb and Cr thresholded per block from the noise model, the Detail blend on luma only) takes the colour grain of 0.5.0 back down, but not everywhere to the old path's level. On the ISO 12800 chart (std of a\*/b\* in flat 8 × 8 blocks): 4.8 against the bilateral path's 4.5 at L\* 15 to 30, 0.7 against 2.2 at L\* 50 to 70, but 6.5 against 4.0 at L\* 30 to 50 (11.1 in 0.5.0) (`docs/algorithms/05-single-frame-denoise.md`, *Measurement*).
- **Very noisy shadows (pipeline 0.5.1).** Method A smooths in the working encoding, so the mean of a noisy shadow comes out a little darker than the scene (the mean of an encoded noisy value lies below the encoding of the mean): about 1 L\* below the bilateral path at L\* 15 to 30 on the ISO 12800 chart. A bias correction at the decoding (the noise model gives the variance per pixel) would remove it.
- **Residency fault test on lavapipe.** `faults::a_device_that_is_out_of_memory_hands_the_group_over_to_the_cpu_and_recovers` (`crates/ym-core/tests/gpu_residency.rs`, feature `fault-injection`) finds 1 of 240,000 bytes one code apart between the device's reduced group and the CPU's, where a software adapter is allowed none. No Linux CI job runs it with `fault-injection`. Not investigated.
- **Colour warp, long narrow moves.** The stepped flow stretches a thin sliver of colours behind the source of a long, narrow move; there the GPU (f32) and CPU (f64) differ by up to about 3e-4 in LogC4 for moves longer than about 0.85 at range 0.01, over the 2e-4 gate. A floor on the range relative to the move (for example |δ|/20) would bound the stretch.
- **Saturation near deep blues.** Positive saturation jumps from the full factor to 1 across Y = 0 for deep blues (blue LEDs), a possible contour in a blue LED gradient at high saturation. A continuous variant is `λ = 1 + (F − 1)·Y/min` for `r < 0`.
- **GPU parity exceptions.** Three parity tests still allow for halo-limiter rank flips (`HALO_FLIP_*` in `crates/ym-core/tests/render_gpu.rs`, the hardware slack of `render_sharpen.rs`); tighten them once a Metal run shows the numbers. The device adaptive ratio demosaic is not bit-identical to the CPU port (1.65e-4 of the samples on real mosaics differ by more than 1e-4, worst 1.55e-2 of white).
- **View against export.** Filmstrip thumbnails do not follow the Output slider, and a 100 % view differs from the export within about 21 px of a crop edge (the view reads photo pixels beyond the crop, the export mirrors at it). A cropped photo in a cell larger than the crop shows uncropped content in the viewport's letterbox; check against `docs/11-ui.md`.
- **Grade controls at opposite extremes.** Two C9 controls combined at opposite extremes (for example Highlights +100 with White −100) can fold the neutral ramp; each alone is monotone. Needs a UI limit or a constants refit.
- **White and estimators.** Day, Shade and Tungsten presets carry the 3.5 to 6 % difference between the Weta curves' white and the camera ColorMatrix white (A7 III, A7 IV). The noise estimator over-reads `a` on busy low-ISO scenes (median 14 %, p90 38 %). The illuminant's synthetic brightness range stops at Bv 13 (real sunlit files read 14.3 to 14.6) and LED and fluorescent recognition is weak on independent chart scenes. The Kelvin readout rounded to 50 K leaves up to 1.6 % red/blue imbalance below 2300 K. The white lift has no local variant and no burst rule, and its row stays visible with an imported ARRI display LUT, where it does nothing.
- **Shared matrices.** 37 of the 44 bodies in `data/cameras/color-matrices.json` fall into 14 groups that carry one published matrix (sensor siblings such as the A7R III and A7R IIIA, the A1 and A1 II); that is right only where the sensor is the same and could not be verified body by body. The A7 V and A7R VI use the A7R IV matrix.
- **Mock and mockups.** The browser mock (`src/api.mock.ts`), the Playwright baselines and the mockups `start.html` and `export.html` draw Neutral as the default look; the app shows Signature. Flipping the mock breaks 34 Vitest tests and the baselines.
- **Look import.** A photo open when an import turns one of the person's own looks into an ARRI look (same bytes, new id) loses its look and its export fails until another look is picked. The wizard's Choose copy says 181 MB for the LogC4 look library (the file is 190 MB), "Open ARRI's page" opens the Look Files page for every package although the two display LUT packages are on ARRI's Technical Downloads page, and the "LogC4 WVO LUT Package" is not recognised. Other Look File limits are in `docs/08-looks.md`.
- **Screenshot references.** The references in `design/screenshots-c2/` were taken on one machine; a CI runner may need a budget for the glass's blur.
